# Hasbreo Message Watch 1.1.0

- Send sounds from your encrypted phone soundboard to a chat.
- Trigger soundboard audio in the active phone call from your watch.
- Private nicknames flow through chat titles and message authors.
- Deleted attachments display a muted red unavailable label.
- Account quick replies are used unless this watch has its own overrides.

Install Message Phone 1.1.0 and approve the watch. Upload/manage sounds on the phone or web. The watch controls the phone’s call; it does not independently capture or stream call audio/video. Physical paired-device testing remains required.
